Tokenizer Fingerprinter

Identify which tokenizer family a model uses (and so, likely, who built it) from the prompt-token counts its API reports. Free models cost nothing; paid models are marked and capped by a per-run spend limit.

The key stays in this browser (localStorage) and is sent only to openrouter.ai.
